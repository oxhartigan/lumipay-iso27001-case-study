# LumiPay ISO/IEC 27001 Case Study (Implementer and Auditor)

> Disclaimer. LumiPay (PT Lumina Bayar Nusantara) is a fictional company created for learning and portfolio purposes only. All company details, data, and personnel are invented. Any resemblance to real organizations, products, or people is coincidental. The auditor exercise was guided, with weaknesses deliberately planted in the documents. This project is not a real client engagement and is not affiliated with any existing company or product.

## What this is

A self-directed case study that works through an ISO/IEC 27001:2022 ISMS for a fictional Indonesian fintech that provides digital payment services for small and medium-sized merchants. I play two roles: first the **implementer** who builds the ISMS documents, then the **auditor** who reviews them and writes findings.

The work follows ISO/IEC 27001:2022, ISO/IEC 27002:2022, and ISO/IEC 27005:2018. The standards themselves are not included in this repository.

## Contents

| Phase | Document | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Implementer | Company Profile and ISMS Scope | Context, interested parties, scope statement and exclusions |
| Implementer | Asset Inventory | Information and associated assets with owners and classification |
| Implementer | Risk Criteria | Likelihood and impact scales, risk levels, acceptance threshold |
| Implementer | Risk Register | 22 risks assessed and prioritized |
| Implementer | Risk workbook (Excel) | Sheets: Risk Register, Risk Criteria, Risk Treatment Plan, Statement of Applicability (93 controls), and summary |
| Implementer | Management Approval | Approval record for the treatment plan and residual risks |
| Auditor | Internal Audit Report | 8 findings (2 Major, 3 Minor, 3 Observations) and follow-up priorities |

## Method

Findings use five parts: Condition, Criteria, Cause, Impact, Recommendation. Documents were read forward to understand the flow, then traced backward to verify, with extra attention to scope exclusions, accepted risks, "not applicable" controls, and residual risk decisions.

## Transparency

The auditor phase was guided. Findings T-01 to T-04 were found and written by me; T-05 to T-08 were drafted with the help of an AI assistant (Claude) from the same document review and then studied by me. The goal is to learn how to review an ISMS, not to claim discoveries in a real system.
